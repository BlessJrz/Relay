## Relay 1.0

Your iMessage chats on the Galaxy Z Fold7, through BlueBubbles Server on your Mac.

**Install:** download `Relay-1.0.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. It installs over any earlier Relay build and keeps your setup.

### What's in it
- **Four designs:** Pinned, Material, Folders and Modern Minimal. Each has its own layout, light/dark mode and accent colours, and works folded or unfolded (edge to edge).
- **Messaging:** swipe a message to reply, double-tap to heart, edit and unsend (Private API, macOS 13+), and send later (hold the send button). You also get voice messages, photos, and videos with thumbnails that play inside Relay. There's a "New messages" line and a jump-to-latest button.
- **Never lose a message:** drafts are kept per chat. Anything you send while the Mac is unreachable waits and goes out by itself when it's back.
- **Notifications:** reply or mark read right from the notification, including from your watch. Mute any chat. Relay warns you if the Mac stops answering.
- **Chats:** pin, mute and mark read or unread by swiping or long-pressing. Each chat has a Photos, videos & links page, and you can save any photo or video to your phone.
- **More:** an optional fingerprint lock, a link preview card for the first link in a message, sharing to Relay from other apps, and an optional NAS archive (`tools/archive`).

Some features need the **Private API** on your Mac (Settings → Mac relay → Private API): reactions, replies, editing, unsending and read receipts.
